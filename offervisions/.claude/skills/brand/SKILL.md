# Offervisions brand

## The show (facts – never invent others)
- **OFFERVISIONS** – a 15-minute self-development podcast on **Big Bang Radio**, a non-commercial college radio station.
- Airs **Mondays at 11 AM**.
- Host: **Lucie**. She is **not** a psychologist or teacher – a **fellow learner** sharing what she is learning about growth. Never present her as an expert, coach or authority.
- Audience: open-minded people of any age who want to grow.
- **2027 is structured like a book**: four seasonal parts + a December epilogue, **one new chapter every two weeks**.

| Part | Season | Theme | Background | Ink (lines, glow, text) |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|------------------------|
| Part One | Winter | Visualisation | `#2E3A6B` | `#FFFFFF` |
| Part Two | Spring | Realisation | *ask Lucie* | *ask Lucie* |
| Part Three | Summer | Connection | *ask Lucie* | *ask Lucie* |
| Part Four | Autumn | Integration | *ask Lucie* | *ask Lucie* |
| Epilogue | December | – | *ask Lucie* | *ask Lucie* |

Only Winter's colours are confirmed. Don't invent the others — ask, then add them to `src/reels/kit/series.ts`.

- Every chapter has **one struggle, one positive, one small challenge**. Challenges are **invitations, not homework**.

## Voice
- Warm, honest, encouraging – like talking to a friend. Never preachy, never clickbait, no hustle-culture language.
- Simple spoken English, short sentences. "I" and "you", not "we should".
- Relatable and humble: "I'm learning this too."
- Words to avoid: *must, should, hack, crush it, grind, guru, secret, life-changing*.

## Visual identity
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920.
- **Colour:** the season colour fills the whole background; *everything else* (lines, glow, text) is the single contrast ink colour. No extra accent colours.
- **Style:** minimalist animated line art, slightly hand-drawn (gentle wobble), lots of empty space, slow calm movement, soft glow on **the one object that matters** in each scene, soft floating light particles, faint paper grain.
- **The horizon line:** one thin continuous line runs through the whole video and transforms into what the story needs (table edge, page edge, path, heartbeat, window, bridge…). It never disappears or cuts.
- **Cozy symbols:** tea cup with steam, lamp, plant, candle, notebook. Personal and warm, never cold or techy.
- **The learner** (recurring character): a simple rounded figure with headphones, carrying a small open book. A stand-in for Lucie and the listener. Friendly dot eyes and a small smile.
- **Signature:** every Reel **opens** on a book whose page turns to show the chapter number, and **closes** with the book shutting softly and the show name.
- **Fresh metaphor:** each chapter (and each Reel) gets its own visual metaphor for the key idea, so Reels don't all look the same.
- Original work only: never copy another creator's videos, characters or layouts.

## Typography
| Use | Font | Style |
|-----|------|-------|
| Small setup line | Instrument Serif | italic, ~58 px |
| Key phrase | Nunito | 900, ALL CAPS, ~92 px, **max 6 words** |
| Tags, labels, handwriting | Caveat | 600–700 |
| Banner / small caps | Nunito | 800, letter-spaced |

## Instagram layout
- **Safe zones:** nothing important in the **top 250 px** or **bottom 400 px** (content lives in y = 250–1520).
- Title banner at the top of the safe area (`OFFERVISIONS · <CHAPTER>`).
- Cover: key content inside the centre 3:4 (y ≈ 240–1680) for the profile-grid crop. **Cover text max 5 words**.

## Captions & hashtags
- Under **80 words**, warm, first person.
- Always end with: `New chapter Monday 11 AM on Big Bang Radio.`
- Then the chapter's hashtags (always starting with `#Offervisions`).

## Audio
- Lucie's own recorded voice is the main sound.
- A few soft sound effects only: page turn, pencil scratch, gentle chime (plus small pops/air).
- Optional quiet instrumental **added in Instagram from its own library**. Never name or embed copyrighted songs.
