# Scriptwriter

Create 30-second videos using:
1. **Relatable tension** in 0–3 seconds
2. **Pattern break** in 3–8 seconds
3. **Insight** in 8–20 seconds
4. **Practical shift** in 20–26 seconds
5. **CTA** in 26–30 seconds

Every line must move the idea forward.
Remove vague statements and generic self-help language.

## Longer briefs (e.g. a 60–75 s Main Reel)
Keep the same five beats in the same proportions and scale them:

| Beat | 30 s | 60 s | 75 s |
|------|------|------|------|
| Tension | 0–3 | 0–6 | 0–7 |
| Pattern break | 3–8 | 6–16 | 7–20 |
| Insight | 8–20 | 16–40 | 20–50 |
| Practical shift | 20–26 | 40–52 | 50–65 |
| CTA | 26–30 | 52–60 | 65–75 |

When Lucia asks for three acts (struggle → turn → small step), map them: **struggle = tension + pattern break**, **turn = insight (with the visual twist)**, **small step = practical shift + CTA**.
Always also write the 30-second version so `social-delivery` can cut it (and a 15 s cut: tension → insight's one line → CTA).

## Hook (0–2 s, inside the tension beat)
Two lines, both readable within ~1 s:
- small *italic* setup line: a specific, relatable moment (not a generic question)
- **BOLD ALL-CAPS** turn of ≤ 6 words that lands the point

e.g. *Your resolution is already slipping?* / **THAT'S NOT FAILURE.**

## Line-by-line rules
- One sentence = one scene. Short. Spoken English.
- Pace: **140–175 words per minute** (30 s ≈ 70–85 words; 60–75 s ≈ 150–180 words).
- Cut any line that only restates, motivates in general ("you've got this"), or could appear in any self-help video.
- Concrete nouns over abstractions: "a sticky note on your mirror" beats "a reminder of your goals".
- Main Reels contain exactly one **[My story here]** slot for one real sentence from Lucia – never invent it.
- The CTA must match what the episode delivers (see `marketing-psychology`).

## Storyboard format
| # | Time | Beat | Voiceover line | What's drawn & how it moves | On-screen text |
- On-screen text: one small *italic* line + at most one **ALL-CAPS phrase ≤ 6 words**.
- Name the beat for each scene so pacing can be checked.
- Write packages to `reels/chNN-<slug>/video-N-<role>.md`: Hook · Voiceover (+30 s and 15 s versions) · Visual idea · Storyboard · Cover · Caption · Audio.

## Chapter set
Video 1 trailer (episode day) · Video 2 Main Reel (Thursday after) · Video 3 follow-up on the challenge. If a brief doesn't define a video's job, use this and state the assumption.
