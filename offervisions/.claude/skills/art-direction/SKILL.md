# Art Direction

> **2027 campaign (current):** a sophisticated independent editorial magazine crossed with contemporary motion graphics and cinematic title sequences. Build with `src/editorial/`: Literata + Inter, the official cover palette (see `brand`), the supplied logo files, film grain, the line of light, page turns. Use symbolism (horizons, doorways, unfolding pages, light, shadow, geometry), never characters or cartoon illustration. Every Reel resolves on its chapter-cover layout as the brand signature.
> The learner, cozy props and hand-drawn wobble described below belong to the retired 2026 Reels (`src/reels/`). Don't use them for 2027 work.

Create an **editorial, cinematic identity.**
Use limited palettes, intentional negative space, and symbolic objects.
Prefer abstract growth, reflection, transition, and human connection metaphors.
Avoid stock-video-style imagery and generic AI illustrations.

Ink is used for every line, glow and piece of text. Hierarchy comes from opacity and glow, not from extra colours.

## Visual language
- Minimal animated line art, slightly hand-drawn (`HandSvg` wobble), faint paper grain, soft floating particles for depth.
- **Negative space:** at least about 40% of the content band stays empty. One focal object per scene.
- **Horizon line:** one thin continuous line runs through the whole video and becomes what the story needs: table edge, page edge, path, heartbeat, window, bridge. It never disappears.
- **Symbolic objects** (cozy, personal, never techy): tea cup with steam, lamp, plant, candle, notebook, plus season icons (snowflake, sprout, bridge, leaf).
- **The learner:** a simple rounded figure with headphones carrying a small open book. A stand-in for Lucia and the listener. Alternative designs (Pebble, Sprout, Page, Little bird, Ember) live in `kit/Avatars.tsx`. The active one is set in `SERIES.learner`, and all Reels share it.
- **Signature:** the video opens on the book turning to the chapter number and closes with the book shutting softly and the show name.
- **Fresh metaphor per chapter and per Reel**, drawn from growth, reflection, transition or connection. Check `reels/` so it doesn't repeat an earlier Reel.
- Original only. Never copy a creator's videos, characters or layouts. No stock-photo clichés (mountain summits, lightbulbs, sunrise runners).

## Cover
The open book showing the chapter number, the learner, and ≤ 5 words, with key content inside the centre 3:4 (y ≈ 240–1680).
