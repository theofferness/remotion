# Ethical Marketing

Identify **one genuine tension** and **one truthful benefit.**
Do not fabricate statistics, testimonials, urgency, or authority.
Avoid fear-based manipulation.
Match the CTA to what the episode actually delivers.

## Applying it to Offervisions
- **Tension:** a real, common feeling the chapter addresses (e.g. "resolutions slipping by mid-January"). Write it down in the package before writing any copy.
- **Benefit:** what a listener actually gets: a 15-minute episode, one idea, one small challenge. Promise reflection or a doable step, not results ("you'll transform your life").
- **Authority:** Lucie is a fellow learner. Don't use "experts say", "science proves" or credentials. When an idea comes from a book or study, name the source in Lucie's words ("I read that…") and only if she supplies it.
- **Numbers:** use them only when true and checkable (e.g. 1.01³⁶⁵ ≈ 37.8 as maths, labelled as an illustration, not a promise). Never invent percentages or listener counts.
- **Urgency:** a real date or time is fine ("Monday 11 AM"). Countdown, scarcity or FOMO language is not.
- **Social proof:** none unless Lucie provides real, consented quotes.
- **CTA ladder** (pick the one that matches the video): listen Monday 11 AM on Big Bang Radio · try this week's small challenge · save this for later · follow for the next chapter. Big Bang Radio is non-commercial, so there are no sales CTAs.
