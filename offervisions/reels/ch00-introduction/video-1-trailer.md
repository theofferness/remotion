## 3 · Visual idea

**One continuous horizon line** runs through every frame and becomes: **table edge → page edge → winding year path → bookshelf → frosted window → table edge.**
The trailer's own metaphor is **the book's table of contents**: the year drawn as a walk past five seasonal stops. Video 2 gets a different metaphor for the key idea.
Signature: it **opens** on a closed book whose page turns to "0 · Introduction", and **closes** as the book shuts softly and the show name appears.
