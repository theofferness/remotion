## 3 · Visual idea
The continuous horizon line becomes **table edge → page edge → winding year path → bookshelf → frosted window → table edge**.
The trailer's metaphor is **the table of contents**: the year as a walk past five seasonal stops.
The book signature opens the video (page turns to **0 · Introduction**) and closes it (book shuts softly, then the OFFERVISIONS end card).
